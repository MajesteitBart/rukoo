You need a workspace where you are an owner, admin or member. Guests talk in channels. [Join a workspace](/chat/join/) first if needed.

1. Open **Chat** and select the workspace.
2. Choose **New message** in the sidebar, or press `Ctrl+N` in Chat.
3. In **To**, pick one to seven other people.
4. Open the conversation. If an open conversation already has exactly those people, **Open conversation** takes you there.
5. Type a message and press `Enter`. Use `Shift+Enter` for a new line.

The conversation opens for everyone in it immediately. A direct message keeps its original participants. Start a new conversation to talk with a different group.

## Close or quiet a conversation

**Close conversation** in its More menu closes posting for everyone in it. Its history remains readable. A closed conversation offers **Start a new conversation** with those people, and **Remove from list**, which removes your access to that history from the list.

To stop your notifications while keeping the conversation open, use [Mute](/chat/notifications-and-blocks/).

You can start 20 new conversations a day. Reopening an existing open conversation does not count toward that limit. A conversation also closes when a participant leaves the workspace, is removed, deletes their account or becomes a guest.
