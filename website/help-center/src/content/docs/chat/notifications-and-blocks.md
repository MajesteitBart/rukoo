## Mute a conversation

Open the conversation's More menu and choose **Mute**. Its row dims, and it stops showing badges or notifications on this device. Set mute separately on each device.

**Settings** > **General** > **Team chat notifications** controls direct-message and mention notifications for the app. Reading a message on another device clears its unread state here too.

Use **Mark as read** in a conversation's More menu, or `Shift+Esc`, to read the whole conversation. **Mark unread** in a message's More menu marks that message and the ones after it unread across devices. Reopen the conversation to resume automatic reading as you scroll.

## Block someone

1. Open your one-to-one conversation, or find the person in **Members**.
2. Choose **Block**.
3. Check which conversations close. Every direct conversation containing both of you closes for everyone, including group conversations.

That person cannot start a direct message with you. Their mentions stop notifying you. You still see each other's posts in shared channels, where their posts still count as unread. The person gets no block notice but may notice the closed conversation.

To reverse the block, open **Workspace settings** > **Blocked people** and choose **Unblock**. Start a new conversation to talk again. The block belongs to this workspace.
