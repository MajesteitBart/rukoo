A channel belongs to one workspace. Owners, admins and members can create one; guests need an invitation to selected channels.

## Create a channel

1. Open **Chat** and the workspace.
2. Select the plus beside **Channels**.
3. Enter a name of 1 to 80 characters on one line.
4. Choose **Everyone in** the workspace or **Only people you add**. Check this choice before creating the channel: its visibility cannot be changed later.
5. Create the channel. You become its manager.

Visible channel names must be unique without regard to upper and lower case.

## Join an existing channel

Open **Browse channels** and find the channel. Open it and choose **Join**. You can read earlier messages after joining. A private channel needs someone to add you.

## Manage or leave

Channel managers can add people, rename the channel, make another person a manager and remove people. Adding a guest requires an owner or admin.

**Leave channel** is in its More menu. If you are its only manager and other people remain, choose a replacement manager first. Leaving a private channel means you need an invitation to return.

**Archive channel** stops posting for all members. Members can read it until retention removes the history. Archiving cannot be undone. The last person to leave also archives the channel.

See [Workspace roles and limits](/chat/roles-and-limits/) for workspace-wide powers.
