As a workspace owner or admin, create a link for one person to join. Use the desktop app's **Chat** tab.

## Make the link

1. Open the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and choose **Invite people**.
2. Choose **Member** or **Guest**. Members can find and join the workspace's visible channels. Guests see only the channels you select.
3. Choose the channels the person joins from the ones you manage. A guest needs at least one. A member also joins the default channel, normally `general`.
4. Choose how long the link works: 1, 3 or 7 days. The default is one day.
5. Choose **Make link**, then **Copy link**.
6. Send the link to that person through a channel you trust.

Rukoo shows the link once. Each link works once. Make a separate link for each person you want to invite.

## Withdraw a link

Open **Open invitations** in the same dialog, or in **Workspace settings**. Choose **Withdraw** beside the invitation you want to stop.

Send your colleague [the first-message tutorial](/chat/join/) with the invitation.
