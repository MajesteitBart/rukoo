Install [Rukoo Voice 0.2.0](/meetings/requirements/) on Windows or Apple Silicon macOS 15+. Dictation works without Rukoo Mail.

## Set up dictation

1. Open Voice's **Settings** and choose **Transcription**.
2. Choose local Orukeet or a speech provider with your own key. Download the local model when it is missing; for a provider, enter its details and save.
3. Check **Dictation key**. On a Mac, grant Microphone and Accessibility access when asked. **Settings** > **Permissions** opens the relevant macOS panes.
4. Focus a text field in the app you want to type into.
5. Hold the dictation key, speak, then release it. The pill shows **Listening**, then **Transcribing**. Check the inserted text.

**Paste mode** inserts through the clipboard and restores its previous contents. It can help in apps where typing is unreliable.

## Rewrite selected text

Configure **Rewriting** and a rewrite key first. Select the text, hold the rewrite key and say what to change. Release it and check the replacement. Without selected text, the pill asks you to select it.

Cloud transcription sends audio to the provider you chose. A local model processes it on this computer. Meeting transcription has its own provider and consent settings.

## If nothing appears

Check the focused text field, microphone and transcription settings. On macOS, missing Accessibility access stops dictation before recording. After an update, review [Voice permissions and recovery](/voice/recovery/).

During a recorded meeting, dictation also reaches the call. Voice marks that stretch in the meeting timeline.
