Turn off **Show in Inbox** for Promotions to read that mail separately. These steps use Rukoo Mail 2.4.0 on desktop, with inbox categories enabled and the default category names.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Inbox categories**.
2. Open the **Promotions** category dialog.
3. Turn off **Show in Inbox**.
4. Return to **Inbox**. Mail in Promotions is excluded from the list. A crossed-out eye marks the category in the sidebar.

Select **Promotions** under Inbox to read its mail and see its own unread count. To put it back in Inbox, turn **Show in Inbox** on again.

The category's unread mail stops contributing to the Inbox count and badge. Older unread messages without a category still count. Search, Unread and VIP views still find this mail, and its notifications continue. Use the separate **Notifications** switch to silence those notifications.

See [Inbox category switches](/mail/category-switches/) for device and sync behavior.
