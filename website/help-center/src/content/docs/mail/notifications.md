Open **Settings** > **General**. **Notifications** controls new-mail notices; **Team chat notifications** controls direct-message and mention notices.

## Test a mail notification

1. Choose **Send a test notification**. This sends a test even when Rukoo's window is in front.
2. Check whether it appears. Rukoo reports whether the system accepted the request; check delivery on your screen too.
3. On macOS, allow Rukoo Mail in **System Settings** > **Notifications**. Ad-hoc signed builds can still fail to show notifications.

New-mail desktop notices appear while Rukoo is in the background. With its window in front, the list and in-window notices show new mail. A category's notification switch also controls its in-window notices. See [Category switches](/mail/category-switches/).

**Sound for new mail** is off by default. Turn it on for one sound when a batch arrives. Rukoo then silences the sound of its mail notifications. The sound pauses while Voice records a meeting.

## Understand badges

The Windows taskbar and macOS Dock badge count mail. By default they count new mail since Rukoo was last in front; reading it or moving it out of Inbox removes it from that count. Linux has no taskbar count badge.

The **Chat** tab counts mentions and unread direct messages across workspaces. A dot means channels have unread messages. To quiet one conversation on this device, [mute it](/chat/notifications-and-blocks/).
