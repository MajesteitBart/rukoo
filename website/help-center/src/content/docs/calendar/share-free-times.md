Rukoo finds open time across the calendars that count toward your availability. Check those calendars before sending someone a list of times.

## Choose the calendars

1. Open **Settings** > **Calendar**.
2. Under **Calendars**, turn **Counts toward availability** on for each calendar whose busy time should block your availability.
3. Check shared calendars too. Counting a colleague's calendar treats their busy time as yours.

Hiding a calendar in the sidebar only hides its events. Its availability setting stays the same.

## Copy free times

1. Open **Calendar** and press `A` to open the availability panel.
2. Choose a meeting length from 15 to 90 minutes.
3. Choose this week, next week or the next 14 days, then the hours and time zone.
4. Check the results. If Rukoo names an account it could not read, reconnect or sync that account before relying on the times.
5. Copy the times as text and paste them into your message.

The calendar grid shows the same open time while the panel is open.

## Insert times while writing mail

In the composer, open the calendar button and choose **Insert my free times…**. Set the length, period, hours and time zone, then read the preview before inserting it.

With **Add my booking link** on, the times use the booking page's hours, the selected meeting type's default duration and minimum notice. The booking page can offer fewer times because it also applies free time around meetings and daily limits. Use the [booking link](/booking/set-up/) when the other person should choose and reserve a time.
