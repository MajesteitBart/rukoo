Install Rukoo Mail to read your mail or join your team's chat.

## Choose a download

Open the [Rukoo Mail 2.4.0 release](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/releases/tag/v2.4.0) and expand **Assets**.

| Your computer | Download | Support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows 10 or 11, x64 | `Rukoo-Mail-Setup-2.4.0.exe` for an installation, or the portable `.exe` | Supported |
| Ubuntu or Debian, x64 | The `.deb` package | Supported |
| Other Linux distributions, x64 | The AppImage | Supported |
| macOS 13 or newer | The DMG or ZIP for Apple Silicon or Intel, to match your Mac | Experimental |

The Windows packages are unsigned. Windows can show a SmartScreen warning. Check the download's source and your organisation's software policy before continuing.

## Open Rukoo

1. Install or open the package for your computer.
2. Start **Rukoo Mail**.
3. At **Set up email**, choose your next task:
   - [Read demo mail](/start/demo-mail/) without credentials.
   - [Connect your mailbox](/mail/connect-account/) with your provider's settings.
   - [Join team chat](/chat/join/) with a colleague's invitation link.

You have reached Rukoo's setup screen. Team chat can be your first task even when you have no mail account in Rukoo.

## Phone and Voice

The 2.4.0 release includes Android 0.6.0, an experimental app for Android 12 or newer, built for the Galaxy Z Fold7. Rukoo Voice is a separate companion for dictation and meetings. Use the [platform guide](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/blob/v2.4.0/docs/platforms.md) for those installations.
