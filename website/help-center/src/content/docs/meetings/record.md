Use [Mail 2.4.0 with Voice 0.2.0](/meetings/requirements/) on the same Windows computer or Apple Silicon Mac. Set up the microphone, call audio and transcription in Voice first. Tell participants how their meeting will be recorded and processed before starting.

## Start the recording

1. Start **Rukoo Voice** yourself.
2. In Rukoo Mail, open **Settings** > **Meetings** and check that Voice is connected.
3. Open **Meet** in the title bar.
4. Open the arrow beside **Record a meeting** and choose **Audio only** for this meeting.
5. Check that the meeting clock starts and the transcript begins to fill as people speak.

For a calendar event, **Record with Rukoo Voice** appears on its card from 15 minutes before the start until the end, while Voice is connected and idle. Its arrow offers the same audio-only choice. Starting there gives the meeting the event's title and links it to the event.

## While recording

Use **Mark** to leave a point in the timeline, **Pause** to pause, and **Resume** to continue. A red dot and clock stay in the title bar in every mode. Select them to return to the meeting.

Voice keeps recording if Mail closes or loses its connection. Reopen Mail to collect the passages it missed. Check Voice's recording state when you intend to finish.

## Stop and review

1. Select **Stop** in the meeting header.
2. Confirm **Stop recording?**.
3. Let processing finish, then open the transcript and [review speakers and notes](/meetings/review/).

If the transcript is waiting, check the transcription settings and any provider-consent prompt in Voice. Recording and transcription have separate states, so keep the recording until you have checked the result.
