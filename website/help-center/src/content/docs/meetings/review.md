Open a recorded meeting under **Meet**. Keep Voice running to listen to audio. Rukoo keeps a transcript copy that you can read while Voice is closed.

## Check the transcript

1. Open the **Transcript** tab.
2. Double-click a passage, or focus it and press `Enter`, to correct its text.
3. Press `Enter` to save or `Esc` to cancel. The passage's right-click menu can restore what Voice recognized.
4. Use the title's edit button, or **Rename meeting** in the sidebar menu, to give the meeting a useful title.

A renamed title stays in Rukoo. Voice's original title stays with its recording.

## Name the voices

1. Open **Details** and find **People**. Voices are grouped under **At your microphone** and **On the call**.
2. Pick a guest from the linked calendar event or type a name for a voice.
3. Use a voice's play button to hear a passage. **Hear each voice** starts with the first unnamed voice; **Assign** names it and moves on.

Playback needs Voice running and a finished recording. During recording, the player shows words without sound because playing a clip would feed it back into the call audio.

If several people shared your microphone, use **Split into voices** after the meeting ends. This needs Voice's speaker model and the meeting's audio. Use **This is me** if Voice chose the wrong microphone voice for you.

Speaker labels separate voices, and can get them wrong. Listen before attaching someone's name to a passage.

## Save your notes

Open **Notes** and write under **Your notes**. The formatting toolbar supports headings, lists, bold text and links. Notes save as Markdown after you stop typing and when you leave the editor. Check for **Saved**.

Saved Markdown has a 100,000-character limit. If **Not saved: shorten the notes** appears, shorten the notes while the app is still open. Rukoo keeps the last saved copy; text beyond the limit stays in the editor until you close the app.

## Ask an agent about the meeting

Open the agent panel while the meeting is on screen. **Catch me up**, **Write the notes** and **Draft the follow-up email** use that meeting's context. Check the output against the transcript before using it.

**Settings** > **Meetings** > **Agents can read meetings** controls access to meeting tools. Switch it off to block that access. Media and audio links also need Voice running.
