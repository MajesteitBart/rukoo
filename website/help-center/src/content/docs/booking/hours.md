Your booking page starts with working hours from **Settings** > **Calendar**. Change the page's schedule when you want a different set of bookable hours.

1. Open **Settings** > **Booking page** on an updated desktop app.
2. Check the page's **Time zone**. Its hours use that zone.
3. Turn on **Use different hours for my booking page** for a separate weekly schedule. Set up to six ranges per day.
4. Under **Dates with other hours**, choose a date to block for the whole day or give different hours. You can set up to 60 dates from today onward.
5. Check the preview on your booking page after the computer that handles bookings publishes the change.

**Block this day** in the calendar's availability panel also creates a date override.

## If a time is missing

Check the calendars counted for conflicts, minimum notice, free time before and after, and daily limits. A calendar hidden in the sidebar can still count for conflicts. Unanswered and Maybe invitations count as busy; a busy all-day event blocks the whole day in the page's zone.

See [Booking rules and limits](/booking/rules/) for meeting-type settings and [How bookings reach your calendar](/booking/how-it-works/) for update timing.
