Turning a page off deletes the page and its bookings from the server. Existing calendar events stay as ordinary events. Review upcoming bookings before you do this, especially ones whose guests may still need their change links.

1. Open **Settings** > **Booking page**.
2. Turn off **Take bookings**.
3. Read the confirmation and confirm when you are ready.
4. Check that the page has stopped taking bookings.

Your page settings remain in your Rukoo account. Turning it on again starts from those settings. The address stays reserved for your account for 12 months.

To cancel a particular meeting and have your calendar send the cancellation, use [Move or cancel a booking](/booking/manage/) before turning the page off.
