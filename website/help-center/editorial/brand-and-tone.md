# Brand and tone

Use Rukoo Mail and Rukoo Voice as product names. The included wordmarks are the released public artwork. Use English and exact English UI labels.

Write directly and warmly. Put the action first. State prerequisites and platform/version limits beside the instruction that depends on them. Plain task titles make pages easier to find in search. An occasional dry line belongs in an introduction only when it helps.

| Write | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Invite a colleague | Grow your flock |
| Turn off **Show in Inbox** | Send Promotions out of the loft |
| The workspace host can read team chat messages. | Your flock is safe in the loft. |
| Explain the action the agent can take and the approval required for that permission level. | The pigeon never gets the keys. |
| The demo account needs no credentials. | Take flight in seconds. |

Keep jokes out of steps, errors, recovery, deletion, privacy, security, permissions and agents acting on mail. Preserve labels even when nearby copy uses ordinary words. Avoid promises such as "secure", "private" or "never" unless the released evidence establishes exactly what the promise covers.

Brand drafts from 2026-11-03 informed these writing rules. Released public product sources establish behavior. The example fixtures follow the v2.4.0 tag rather than current main or nightly.

## Help-center colors

The help center follows the [live Rukoo website](https://rukoo.io/) and its [public stylesheet](https://rukoo.io/styles.css), checked 2026-11-25. The stylesheet defines `--green: #16f0a3` and uses it for focus outlines and action-chip icons. The site's neck gradient combines that green with turquoise, violet and magenta. These are colors in use on the website.

The help center keeps `#16f0a3` as its dark-theme accent. Its light-theme controls use the darker green `#087e5b` for contrast on white. Pale and deep supporting shades keep links, focus states and selected navigation readable. Treat the live CSS as the reference for this palette; the writing guidance above covers tone.
