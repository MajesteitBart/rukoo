Questions about paying.

Who counts as a member?

Everyone who joined the workspace, the owner included. An invitation that nobody has accepted yet costs nothing.

What if we use more than the allowance?

Nothing extra is charged unless the workspace owner allows it. That spending limit starts at €0.

Do I need Rukoo Cloud to use Rukoo?

No. Mail, calendar, meetings, Rukoo Voice and your own agents work without it. For team chat you can also run the open-source server on your own Cloudflare account.

Can you read our team chat?

Yes. Chat messages and files aren't end-to-end encrypted, so the server that runs the workspace can read them. What your Rukoo account syncs between your devices is end-to-end encrypted. The privacy statement has the details.
