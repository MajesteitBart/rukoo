Version 1.2.0 made the assistant chat yours to arrange, and it made mail open faster.

A new chat offers shortcut buttons such as "Draft a reply" and "Brief me". In this version you could arrange them in Settings. You could move them up or down, hide one from new chats, give one a new name, or change the instructions it sends to the assistant. You could also add your own, with its own name, picture and instructions. A hidden shortcut still worked if you typed its command. Rukoo kept up to 40 shortcuts, and they worked the same with every assistant.

![Settings for arranging chat shortcuts](/img/changelog/v1.2.0-1-settings-for-arranging-chat-shortcuts.png)

*The shortcut list in Settings, with one changed, one hidden and one added by the user. Source: [issue #60](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/issues/60).*

Your assistants have skills of their own, on top of the ones Rukoo brings. Think of a skill as a practiced routine, such as preparing for a meeting in a set way. In 1.2.0 you could type the dollar sign in the chat box to see the skills of the assistant in that chat, pick one, and send it with your message. The skill appeared in your message as a small label with its name, so you could see what you had asked for. Rukoo's own skills kept their slash commands, such as typing a name after a slash to start one.

![The dollar-sign menu with an assistant's skills](/img/changelog/v1.2.0-2-the-dollar-sign-menu-with-an-assistant-s-s.png)

*Typing the dollar sign shows the skills of the assistant in this chat. Source: [issue #60](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/issues/60).*

Mail itself got quicker in three ways. A long email with pictures used to stay squashed into a thin strip until the last picture on the internet had loaded. Now it showed at full height right away. After each check for new mail, Rukoo quietly downloaded your newest 30 inbox messages in the background, without marking them as read, so opening them felt instant. A message that was not downloaded yet fetched itself on its own line, instead of waiting behind a check for new mail. Checks also asked the mail service only for what could have changed, so they finished much sooner.

![A long email one second after opening it](/img/changelog/v1.2.0-3-a-long-email-one-second-after-opening-it.png)

*One second after opening a long email. Before this version, the same mail was still a thin strip at this point. Source: [issue #55](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/issues/55).*

Last, Rukoo could open email links from other apps. If you clicked an email address in your web browser, Windows could open a Rukoo write window with the address, subject and text already filled in.

![Write window opened from an email link in another app](/img/changelog/v1.2.0-4-write-window-opened-from-an-email-link-in.png)

*A write window opened from an email link, with the details filled in. Source: [issue #54](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/issues/54).*
