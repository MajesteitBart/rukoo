Version 1.6.0 changed nothing on the computer. It polished the phone, and it put Rukoo under the MIT license. That is a short, permissive open-source license. In short, other people may use, copy and build on the code, as long as they keep the license note.

On the phone, the chat shortcuts became editable, just as on the computer. The round button after the shortcuts in a new chat used to open Settings without a way to edit anything. Now it opened the shortcut page. You could move shortcuts up and down, take one out of new chats, rename one or rewrite its instructions, and add your own. The phone kept its own list.

![Chat shortcuts on the phone's small screen](/img/changelog/v1.6.0-1-chat-shortcuts-on-the-phone-s-small-screen.png)

*Editing chat shortcuts on the small outer screen. Source: [issue #114](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/issues/114).*

Reading mail got easier on the thumb. Previous and Next moved from a menu at the top of the screen to the bottom bar, at the bottom right, next to the assistant button. They grey out at the first and last mail. When the bar runs out of room, Reply shows only its picture and a long assistant name gets shortened, so nothing falls off the edge.

![Previous and Next in the bottom bar](/img/changelog/v1.6.0-2-previous-and-next-in-the-bottom-bar.png)

*Previous and Next where your thumb already is, in the reader's bottom bar. Source: [issue #113](https://github.com/MajesteitBart/rukoo/issues/113).*
