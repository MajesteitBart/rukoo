Use a [connected calendar](/calendar/connect/) that you can write to. Google calendars support guests. CalDAV guests need a server that sends invitations.

## Create an event

1. Open **Calendar** and choose the week or day view.
2. Drag across the time you want. A small event form opens beside the grid.
3. Enter a **Title** and choose the **Calendar** that should hold the event.
4. Add guests if needed. Check the addresses before saving. On Google, choose whether Google should email them; a CalDAV server that sends invitations emails guests about changes automatically.
5. For a place or description, select **More options**. The full dialog has **Location** and **Description**. Open **More** for repeats, reminders and the event's time zone.
6. Save the event. In the small form, `Enter` saves it.

The event appears at the chosen time. Open its card to check the calendar, guests and time. A Google Meet link can take a few seconds to appear.

## Move or resize it

Drag an event to another time or day in week or day view. Drag its bottom edge to change its length. In month view, drag it to another day.

Use the notice's **Undo** if you moved or resized it by mistake. For a recurring event, choose whether the change applies to this event, this and following events, or all events when Rukoo asks.

## If someone changed it elsewhere

Rukoo shows the latest event with your edits on top when the provider reports a change during editing. Read the form again before saving. This protects the other person's change from being overwritten without a check.

For a booked event, follow [Move or cancel a booking](/booking/manage/) so you can check the effect on the guest's booking too.
